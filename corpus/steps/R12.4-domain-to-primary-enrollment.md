OutGuess produced two signed 22-position book codes. “A poem of fading death, named for a king / Meant to be read only once and vanish” selected William Gibson's *Agrippa* and resolved exactly to `sq6wmgv2zcsrix6t.onion`. “In twenty-nine volumes, knowledge was once contained” is commonly associated with the Britannica `CICADA` article; the preserved source resolves 18 of 22 positions and leaves its exact edition open.

It instructed the solver to create a never-used public webmail address while using Tor, submit it, wait for a number, then revisit the same onion at `/<number>`; the example number was `3894894230934209`.

**State handed forward.** Primary enrollment yields a fresh address, a received number and a private path for R12.5; the numbered poster identifiers remain available to the R12.6 branch.
