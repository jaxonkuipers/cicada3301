# R13.2 — ISO, audio and Twitter to Gematria Primus

R13.1's `761.MP3` was titled `The Instar Emergence`, artist `3301`. Appended bytes contained `Parable 1,595,277,641` and the poem “Like the instar, tunneling to the surface / We must shed our own circumferences; / Find the divinity within and emerge.” The Twitter account emitted offset-labelled 65-byte hex blocks; ordering them by offset and XORing the byte stream with the exact MP3 recovered the Gematria Primus rune-table JPEG.

The [reconstructed scan and machine-readable table](artifacts/gematria-primus/) define 29 runes, transliterations, values and prime ordering. The scan prints `U`; the table's U/V alias is a transcription convention. The poem's three line sums are 1259, 1031 and 1229, whose product equals the printed parable number.

Gematria Primus passes to [R13.3](../R13.3/) and later routes. The authored word `DIVINITY` remains available and becomes the selected 2014 key at R14.3. The original Twitter state and timing observations are unavailable; the reconstructed stream and scan survive.
