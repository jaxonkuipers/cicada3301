# R15.1 — denial of illegal attribution

After news reports connected “3301” and Cicada symbolism with a group claiming attacks against Planned Parenthood, Cicada published a [signed statement](artifacts/communications/2015-07-planned-parenthood-denial.asc) on 28 July 2015. It said, “We do not engage in illegal activities,” denied association with the group and rejected its use of Cicada's name, number and symbolism.

The signature verifies under Cicada's established OpenPGP identity. The statement reasserts the identity and legality boundary established independently in [R12.8](../R12.8/). The exact signed bytes survive; the original publication context is incomplete.
