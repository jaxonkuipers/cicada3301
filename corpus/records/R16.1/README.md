# R16.1 — explicit direction to Liber Primus

With Liber Primus unresolved and unsigned continuations circulating, Cicada signed a [new message](artifacts/communications/2016-01-liber-primus.asc) at `2016-01-01 00:01:07Z`: `The path lies empty; epiphany seeks the devoted. Liber Primus is the way. Its words are the map, their meaning is the road, and their numbers are the direction. Seek and you will be found.` It ends `Beware false paths. Verify OpenPGP 7A35090F.`

The message directly assigns roles to the book's words, meaning and numbers while retaining OpenPGP as the provenance boundary. Liber Primus therefore remains the active public route surface passed from R14.7. The exact representations and operations denoted by those three terms remain unresolved.
