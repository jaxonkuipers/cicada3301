# R12.7 — close of the first public round

On 6 February 2012, after the individualized [R12.5](../R12.5/) route and [R12.6](../R12.6/) second-chance route had moved beyond public view, Cicada published a [signed closing message](artifacts/communications/2012-02-end-of-puzzle.asc). It said it had found the individuals it sought, thanked participants, ended the month-long journey and promised future opportunities. A large decimal postscript closes the signed body.

The message closes the public 2012 round and asserts that selection occurred. The surviving record leaves the selected identities, criteria, accepted assessment state and subsequent recipient activity unknown. The operational purpose of the decimal postscript is also unresolved.

The signature verifies under the key introduced at R12.2. Later public rounds fulfill the promise of future opportunities, while R13.1 begins a separate public route in 2013.
